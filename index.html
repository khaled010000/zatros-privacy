Privacy Policy (English)

Effective: November 22, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Zatros Pro (package ID: com.aistudio.rewardbox.qzvkpm). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data you share when using our Android application, games, voice rooms, community features, and rewards store. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, which permissions and third-party services we rely on, and how you can delete your account and data at any time.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account & Authentication Information: When you sign in using Google Sign-In, Email & Password, or Anonymous Guest login, we process your Firebase User ID (UID), email address, display name / username, profile avatar selection, and authentication provider metadata.
  • Gameplay, Points & Store Redemption Data: We store your points balance, chronological points ledger (earned from rewarded ads, daily streaks, games, referrals, or admin adjustments), daily ad counters, leaderboard rank, community donations, and store redemption orders (including any game player ID or recipient account identifier you voluntarily submit so we can fulfill digital rewards).
  • Community & Multiplayer Room Data: Messages sent in public chat, private direct chats, order support chats, and active multiplayer game rooms (such as Dominoes, Rock-Paper-Scissors, Guess What I Have, Mafia, and social party games) are synchronized through Cloud Firestore so players can interact in real time. User-submitted safety blocks and abuse reports are also stored for moderation.
  • Device, Diagnostic & Anti-Fraud Data: Basic technical metadata such as app version code, network connectivity status, Firebase Analytics usage events, and Firebase App Check (reCAPTCHA) integrity signals to prevent fraud, unauthorized modified versions, and points abuse.

3. App Permissions & How They Are Used

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO), Audio Settings (MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS) & Bluetooth (BLUETOOTH_CONNECT): Used strictly for optional real-time voice communication inside multiplayer game rooms (powered by Agora RTC) and optional voice queries in the in-app FAQ helper.
    Strict No-Recording Guarantee for Voice Rooms
    Your microphone is muted by default whenever you enter a game room and is only activated when you grant permission and manually press the microphone toggle button. Real-time voice audio in game rooms is streamed live during gameplay only and is never recorded, saved, or stored on our servers.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): Used by Google AdMob and mediated advertising partners (such as Unity Ads) to deliver rewarded video ads, prevent ad fraud, cap frequency, and personalize or measure ad performance in accordance with your device privacy settings.
  • Internet & Network Access (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, ACCESS_WIFI_STATE): Required to authenticate users, sync points and orders securely with Cloud Firestore, verify active online status before crediting ad rewards, and operate online multiplayer games.
  • Notifications & Vibration (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, VIBRATE): Used to deliver optional notifications (such as order review updates, daily reward alerts, and new store items) and tactile gameplay feedback. You can manage or disable alerts inside Settings.

5. Data Retention, Security & Children's Privacy

  • Security: All communication between the app and Firebase / Agora / AdMob servers is encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS. Server-side Firestore Security Rules enforce strict per-user access controls and version validation.
  • Data Retention: We retain your account and points ledger while your account remains active so you do not lose your earned points or redemption records across devices.
  • Children's Privacy: Zatros Pro is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us immediately at Zatrosp@gmail.com so we can remove it.

6. Account & Data Deletion

How to Permanently Delete Your Account & Data

You have full control over your account and personal information:

  • Clear Local Device Data & Sign Out: Inside the app, go to Profile → Log Out to end your session, or uninstall the app / clear app storage in Android Settings to remove all local Room database caches and preferences.
  • Request Permanent Cloud Account & Data Deletion: To permanently delete your Firebase Authentication account and all associated Cloud Firestore records (profile, points ledger, chat messages, referrals, and order history), send an email from your registered email address (including your Display Name or Account Numeric ID) to our official support team:
Request Deletion: Zatrosp@gmail.com

Verified deletion requests are processed within 7 business days (and no later than 30 days), after which your authentication credentials and personal database records are permanently erased, except where minimal records must be retained to comply with legal obligations or prevent severe abuse/fraud.

7. Policy Updates & Contact Us

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. If you have questions or privacy requests, contact us at: Zatrosp@gmail.com.

  • الميكروفون (RECORD_AUDIO) وإعدادات الصوت (MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS) والبلوتوث (BLUETOOTH_CONNECT): تُستخدم حصراً للمحادثة الصوتية الفورية داخل غرف الألعاب الجماعية (عبر Agora RTC) أو عند إرسال استفسار صوتي اختياري داخل مساعد الأسئلة الذكي.
    تعهد صريح بعدم تسجيل أو تخزين الصوت
    يكون الميكروفون مكتوماً افتراضياً لحظة دخولك لأي غرفة لعب، ولا يعمل إلا بعد موافقتك على الإذن وضغطك يدوياً على زر فتح المايك. يتم نقل الصوت بشكل فوري (بث حي) أثناء اللعب فقط ولا يتم تسجيله أو حفظه أو تخزينه إطلاقاً على خوادمنا.
  • معرّف الإعلانات (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): يُستخدم بواسطة خدمة Google AdMob والشركاء المعتمدين (مثل Unity Ads) لعرض إعلانات المكافآت، ومنع الاحتيال الإعلاني، وتحديد سقف التكرار وفق إعدادات الخصوصية بجهازك.
  • الوصول للإنترنت وحالة الشبكة (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, ACCESS_WIFI_STATE): ضروري لتسجيل الدخول، ومزامنة الرصيد والطلبات مع Cloud Firestore، والتحقق من الاتصال الفعلي قبل احتساب نقاط الإعلانات، وتشغيل الألعاب الأونلاين.
  • الإشعارات والاهتزاز (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, VIBRATE): لإرسال تنبيهات اختيارية حول حالة الطلبات والمكافآت ومنتجات المتجر وتوفير اهتزاز تفاعلي داخل الألعاب، ويمكنك التحكم بها أو إيقافها من الإعدادات.
